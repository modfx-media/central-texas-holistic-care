# SEO audit: Central Texas Holistic Care

**Site:** https://centraltexasholisticcarepllc.com  
**Period:** June 25–September 23, 2026  
**Sources:** Google Search Console (property `https://centraltexasholisticcarepllc.com/`), Google Maps local pack, on-page review of the live Next.js site  
**Prepared:** September 25, 2026

## Bottom line

The site is indexed. Google is already showing it for the searches that should become patients. Those listings are not getting clicked, and the hormone-therapy map pack is going to other Killeen clinics.

In 90 days the site recorded **233 clicks**, **8,532 impressions**, a **2.7% click-through rate**, and an impression-weighted average position of **18.3**.

| Device | Clicks | Impressions | Average position |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Mobile | 138 | 4,032 | 10.1 |
| Desktop | 93 | 4,458 | 25.6 |
| Tablet | 2 | 42 | 20.3 |

The homepage alone had 133 clicks and 3,440 impressions (position 11.8). The only query that reliably earns a click is the brand name, “central texas holistic care” (18 clicks, position 3.9).

## Why leads are going to competitors

### 1. Page-one listings with a 0% click-through rate

| Query | Where it ranks | Impressions | Clicks | Position |

| holistic medicine killeen | Homepage | 65 | 0 | 2.5 |

| hormone therapy bell county | Temple perimenopause page | 65 | 0 | 3.1 |

| bioidentical hormones killeen | Killeen hormone page | 30 | 0 | 7.4 |
| hormone therapy killeen tx | Split across several URLs | 59 | 0 | 18.7 |

People see the clinic and choose another result. The homepage title said **Harker Heights**. The address Google, the footer, and the schema all use is **311 E. Stan Schlueter Loop #207, Killeen, TX 76542**. The hero headline was a slogan and did not name Killeen or the service.

### 2. The hormone map pack belongs to other clinics

For “hormone therapy killeen tx,” the Google Maps top 3 is:

1. Empower Wellness Clinic — 5.0 from 8 reviews, Killeen, booking on
2. Wellstone Health Partners — 3.1 from 12 reviews, listing not claimed
3. Chris Glow Body & Wellness — 5.0 from 14 reviews, Killeen

Central Texas Holistic Care is not in that pack.

For “holistic medicine killeen,” the clinic **is** Maps #1, at **3.8 stars from 18 reviews**, including five 1-star reviews. The next two listings are 5.0 (Metanoia Mantra, 19 reviews; Rosa’s Natural Healing Center, 203 reviews). The #1 spot is held by the Killeen address, not by review strength.

Organic results for “hormone doctor killeen” are led by national directories (BioTE, Klinic, MediFind). The clinic’s Killeen hormone page is indexed and sits around position 6.

### 3. 817 URLs are competing with each other

The original rollout plan was about 13 URLs. All 30 cities are live, including Waco, Georgetown, Round Rock, Cedar Park, Leander, and Taylor. The Waco hormone page is indexed. Search Console is full of impressions for places the clinic does not serve (Marshall, Cedar Lake, Center TX, Frisco, Austin) and those impressions do not click.

“Hormone therapy bell county” is split across Temple, Belton, and Killeen URLs, so no single page owns the click.

Impressions jumped from about 100 a week to about 1,000 a week at the end of July, when this page set started showing. Clicks stayed in the tens.

## Keywords

The topics are right: holistic medicine, hormone therapy, bioidentical HRT, functional medicine, and testosterone. “Functional medicine near me” is about 14,800 US searches a month, and the site already appears around positions 4–6. City-level phrases such as “hormone therapy killeen” are too small to show Google Ads volume. Search Console impressions are the real local demand.

What was wrong was the city in the title. Harker Heights should stay as a city the clinic serves. Killeen is the address and the query.

## Team content

| Issue | What the site said | Why it matters |

## What was changed on September 25

Titles and descriptions on the homepage, the site default, the hormone therapy page, and the IV pages now lead with Killeen and mention Harker Heights as a served city. The homepage hero now says Killeen and “providers,” and the first heading is “Holistic Medicine and Hormone Therapy in Killeen, TX.”

Fifteen distant city pages are unpublished (Waco, Hewitt, Woodway, Lorena, Georgetown, Round Rock, Leander, Cedar Park, Liberty Hill, Florence, Taylor, Burnet, Marble Falls, Evant, and Lometa). Bell and Coryell County pages stay live. Each remaining city and service page now includes a visit section and a candidacy section, plus two local FAQs. Each treatment page now opens with a three-paragraph narrative tied to that city, service, and protocol.

Bimisa Augustin and Larissa Garth each have a profile at `/providers/bimisa-augustin/` and `/providers/larissa-garth/`. The about page links to both. Combined experience on the about page is now 36 years (26 and 10+). User-facing “physician-supervised” lines now say clinician-supervised. Larissa’s PA-C credential is unchanged.

These pages stay on the designed layout. Nothing was bulk-published over them.

## What still needs a decision

Raise the Google rating. That is what is keeping hormone-therapy map leads with the 5.0 clinics. It cannot be fixed in the website code.
